Visual Grammar

Every outfit should tell a story about who I am.

Rigid & Defined

Heavy wools, crisp poplins, structured leather — fabrics that hold their shape and answer for themselves.

Fluid & Kinetic

Matte silk, heavy crepe, fine linen — textures that cascade and react to motion, never rigid, never still.

A curated juxtaposition of both structural and fluid elements.

The Wardrobe Commandments

Tell a story.

Every outfit should tell a story about who I am. My clothing is an extension of my personality, not just something I wear.

Dress with intention.

Always dress with intention and have fun with it. Let your personality shine, embrace a touch of whimsy, and never be afraid to express your own unique sense of style.

Know what works.

Understand the silhouettes, fabrics, and colors that complement your body and complexion, and wear them with confidence.
