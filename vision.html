Strategic Innovation

Hermela's approach to leadership rejects incremental thinking. She disrupts standard patterns, introduces novel concepts, and challenges assumptions.

Innovation, in her framework, is not about being first—it's about being intentional. Every decision, every strategy, every action serves a purpose larger than efficiency metrics.

Humanistic Visionary

Behind every system, every project, every initiative is a human story. Hermela leads with this core conviction: "Influence is a tool best used to empower others, challenge perspectives, and create meaningful change."

She prioritizes human connection, mentorship, and impactful storytelling. Technology and fashion are tools—the real work is building people.

Challenging Narratives

ON TECHNOLOGY
