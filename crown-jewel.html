More Than a Title

Pageantry, for Hermela, is not about external validation or fleeting glory. It is a platform for amplifying her voice and her vision. As Miss Tourism Ethiopia and a runner-up at Miss Ethiopia 2026, she carries the responsibility of representing not just a nation, but a philosophy: that a woman can be intellectual and elegant, strategic and graceful, powerful and vulnerable.

At Miss Tourism Africa in Nigeria this September, Hermela will represent Ethiopia with the same intentionality she brings to everything: as an ambassador of innovation, human potential, and uncompromising authenticity. This is her stage. This is her moment to shift the narrative of what beauty, intelligence, and leadership look like on the global stage.
